00 · What you get

Three ways a partnership pays

One USDT deposit does three things: it buys you a vesting ORBIX allocation, it pays a referral up your sponsor tree, and it feeds a global profit pool that active partners share by tier. The deposit itself splits 5% global pool · 10% referral · 40% liquidity · 20% trading · 25% operations.

◇Your ORBIX allocation

USDT converts to ORBIX at a fixed $0.025 (orbix = usdt × 40). It's reserved for you at deposit from the contract's ORBIX, locked 6 months, then released 2.5% every 30 days.

  • Fixed price — no market timing.
  • Fully backed the moment you deposit.
  • Withdrawn with claim / claimAll.

%Referral income

Every deposit beneath you earns USDT up the sponsor tree: 5% to the direct sponsor, then 1% for five more levels — held for you until you collect it.

  • Paid in USDT, six levels deep.
  • Held per upline; collect with claimReferral → your vault balance.
  • Levels with no upline roll into operations.

★Global profit share

5% of every deposit feeds a global pool split across four tiers. Your tier is set by how much ORBIX you still hold; within your tier you share pro-rata by holding. It's a monthly dividend — collect once per calendar month (the window resets on the 1st, 00:00 UTC).

  • Bronze 10% · Silver 20% · Gold 30% · Platinum 40%.
  • Pro-rata by ORBIX holding within your tier.
  • claimGlobal once per month → your vault balance.
01 · The vesting schedule

Six-month cliff, then 2.5% every 30 days for 40 months

Your allocation is locked for a 6-month (180-day) cliff. The first slice releases 30 days after the cliff (day 210), then 2.5% every 30 days until 100% has been returned — 40 slices in all. A "month" is always exactly 30 days. Collect one slice or many at once — accumulated slices are always claimable.

1Deposit

Pay USDT (min $10). You receive usdt × 40 ORBIX, reserved and locked. A vesting position opens with lockEnd = now + 180 days.

2Cliff

For 180 days nothing releases — _vested returns 0 before lockEnd. Your allocation still counts toward your tier and global-pool share.

3Release

From day 210, 2.5% / 30 days (VESTING_BPS = 250). Cumulative vested is M × 2.5% × slices, capped at your allocation M.

4Complete

After 40 slices (≈ month 46 from deposit) the full allocation is yours and the position closes. As you claim, your holding falls — and your tier can too.

The first release lands 30 days after the cliff because slices count from lockEnd: slices = (now − lockEnd) / 30d, so slice 1 needs now ≥ lockEnd + 30d. Total lifecycle: 6-month cliff + 40 monthly slices ≈ 46 months.

03 · Where a deposit goes

Every deposit splits 5 / 10 / 40 / 20 / 25

The contract routes each USDT deposit five ways. The 5% global pool and the 10% referral stay in the contract until members collect them (both are then credited to the member's vault balance); liquidity / trading / operations leave at deposit time.

The deposit split

Global pool 5% (stays) Referral 10% Liquidity 40% Trading 20% Operations 25%

Set by setSplit(global, referral, liquidity, trading) — operations is the remainder. Referral is divided per level by setReferralLevels (default 5% + 1%×5 = 10%).

Where each slice goes

  • 5% global — kept in the contract, accrued to the four tier sub-pools; claimed via claimGlobal → the member's vault balance.
  • 10% referral — held per upline as USDT at deposit; collected via claimReferral → the member's vault balance; unfilled levels go to operations.
  • 40% liquidity — added to the ORBIX/USDT pool via liquidity.addLiquidityUSDT (held in liquidityBuffer if the LP isn't ready yet).
  • 20% trading → the tradingWallet; 25% operations → the operationWallet. Both default to the deployer and are reassignable.

Self-custody & ORBIX backing

  • The admin funds the contract by transferring ORBIX to it; each deposit reserves its allocation.
  • A deposit reverts UNBACKED if the ORBIX balance can't cover all outstanding allocations plus the new one.
  • Invariant: ORBIX.balanceOf(this) ≥ totalOutstandingOrbix — always. Program cap: 100,000,000 ORBIX.

Member USDT stays

After a deposit, the contract holds the 5% global pool and the 10% held referral (plus any liquidity that couldn't be routed yet); trading and operations have already left. When a member collects, that USDT moves into the OrbixAssets vault and is credited to their balance. USDT can't be rescued out — it's member money, and the liquidity buffer has a bounded withdraw.

04 · Tiers & the global pool

Hold more ORBIX, earn a bigger share

The 5% global pool is split into four tier sub-pools — Bronze 10% · Silver 20% · Gold 30% · Platinum 40%. Your tier is decided by the ORBIX you currently hold (allocated minus what you've claimed). Deposit more to rank up; as you claim over 40 months your holding falls, so a partner naturally ranks down and eventually out. Within a tier, members share that sub-pool pro-rata by holding.

Floors set by setTierFloors; sub-pool weights by setTierPoolBps (must sum to 100%). A holding below 40,000 ORBIX earns no global share. If a tier currently has no members, that sub-pool's slice rolls into operations. A depositor's brand-new allocation doesn't earn from its own deposit's global cut — only holdings that were already active do. Collection is monthly: claimGlobal can be called at most once per calendar month — the window resets at 00:00 UTC on the 1st (currentEpoch = year*12 + month-1) per member; each calendar month's pool is snapshotted in monthlyGlobalPool. Your accrual is continuous and can't be front-run — the monthly gate only sets how often you sweep it to your vault balance. (The vesting release schedule is separate and stays on 30-day slices.)

06 · Built to be safe

Where your value lives — and what can't touch it

🔒Reserved at deposit

Your ORBIX allocation is backed the instant you deposit — the contract must already hold enough ORBIX to cover every outstanding allocation, or the deposit reverts UNBACKED.

$Member USDT stays funded

The 5% global pool and 10% held referral stay in the contract until members collect, so they always back claimGlobal and claimReferral. Trading and operations leave at deposit time and never touch them.

⛓Credited to your vault

Collected referral + global USDT is moved into the OrbixAssets vault and credited to your balance. This needs the contract to be an accounts admin — a privilege it uses only to call creditBalance; its code never moves staking or membership principal.

♺Rescue is fenced

rescueToken can move ORBIX only above the reserved (outstanding) amount, and USDT not at all. The only USDT that leaves via admin is the bounded withdrawLiquidityBuffer / withdrawGlobalUndistributed.

⏸Pause protects entries

Deposits and pool top-ups can be paused in an emergency, but claims stay open — members can always release vested ORBIX and claim their referral/global USDT.
