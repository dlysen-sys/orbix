01 · The ladder

Ten ranks, 5% to 50%

Your rank is set by three things: your group sales volume (all the USDT staked in your downline), your count of active direct referrals, and your own minimum self-stake (your live open position). Each rank up is +5% to your override rate. Rank is a high-water mark — once reached it's kept — but your earning is re-priced live by your self-stake (below).

RankTitleGroup salesActive directsMin self-stakeOverride rate

Thresholds are the rankFor ladder; the min self-stake is minStakeForRank(r); the rate is rateForRank(r) = r × 5%. Group sales roll up the binary tree; the active-direct count is on your affiliate tree. An admin can assign a rank with setRankFloor — it overrides these rules (final rank = whichever is higher, natural or assigned) and earns even with no self-stake. The thresholds themselves (group volume, directs, min self-stake) are admin-tunable state via setRankRequirement — the promotion lever; the values shown are the current defaults.

02 · The daily override

A passive yield on each leg

For each leg the contract computes a daily amount and lets it accrue over time — the volume can sit still and your earnings keep climbing every day. The formula, exactly as the contract runs it:

daily = legSales × multiplier × ( yourRate − downlineRate )
then pending = daily × days since you ranked up or last claimed

Achieved % = your rate − the strongest downline's rate on that leg. Over a non-ranking leg it's your full rate. yourRate is your earning rank's rate — throttled by your self-stake, or lifted by an admin rank assignment (see §04). The multiplier is a program-set daily factor (the contract default is 0.5%; the program tunes it to target a sustainable daily rate) — drag it to see the effect. You run the same math on your other leg, and claim each independently.

03 · Breakaway

You earn as long as you're the higher rank

Your override on a leg is a differential — your rate minus the strongest ranked leader below you. As your downline grows their own rank, your share of that leg narrows. When a downline reaches your rank, that leg's differential hits zero: it has broken away, and you stop earning new income from it. Income ends only this way — never through a demotion.

◆Non-ranking leg → full rate

A leg with no ranked leader below pays your entire rank rate. A rank-10 leader over a fresh leg earns the full 50% differential every day.

◑Downline ranks up → you narrow

When a leader below reaches, say, rank 4 (20%), your differential drops to your rate − 20%. You keep everything you already accrued — only the forward rate changes.

◯Equal rank → breakaway

When a downline matches your rank, that leg's differential is 0. It breaks away for good. When both legs have broken away, your override income is zero — but your rank stays.

04 · The safety net

You earn only while you're staked

Rank and volume are kept forever — but earning is not automatic. Every stream (override, referral, and your own staking passive) pays only while you hold a live open position. This keeps income tied to real, committed capital.

◈Live position required

  • You earn while activeStake > 0 (a live open position) — or the admin has assigned your rank (rankFloor > 0).
  • Close your stake and override, referral, and passive all pause the same block — until you re-stake.
  • Reviving requires re-staking ≥ your prior amount — you can't downgrade a lapsed stake back on.

◑Min self-stake throttles the rate

  • Each rank needs its min self-stake (the ladder in §01) to earn at that rate.
  • Withdraw below the floor and your earning rank re-prices down to the rank your remaining stake backs — the title is kept (monotonic), the earning isn't.
  • Admin override: setRankFloor assigns a rank that beats the rules and earns even with no self-stake — final rank = max(natural, assigned).

The exact gate in the contract: _dailyRate returns 0 when activeStake == 0 && rankFloor == 0, and otherwise prices you at min( cached rank , max( stake-backed rank , rankFloor ) ). The same live-stake test guards claimPassive / releaseHeld and the referral payout.

  • Left and right accrue and claim independently — two claim buttons.
  • Claiming (or ranking up) resets that leg's day counter; whatever accrued is kept.

No demotion, ever

  • Rank is a high-water mark — once you reach it you hold it forever, even if downline members restructure.
  • A downline unstaking never claws back your rank, your leg volume, or your accrued income.
  • Income only ever winds down through breakaway, reaching zero only once both legs have broken away.
  • Your accrued override is booked at the live rate — a later breakaway can never reduce what's already yours.
06 · Funding & safety

Backed by the pool, protected for members

◈Pool-funded

Every USDT stake routes a leadership slice into the override pool. Your claimed income is a backed balance in that pool until you withdraw it.

⛓Binary genealogy

Volume rolls up the binary tree (your two legs) via recordStake, depth-capped so no walk can be gassed out.

🛡Never over-pays

The breakaway tracker only ever tightens as a leg strengthens, so the contract can never pay a differential larger than you've truly earned.

The override is a daily yield, so the program keeps the pool funded from staking's leadership slice; an admin sweep can never touch USDT owed to members' held balances. The multiplier is a tunable program parameter (setOverrideMultiplier, default 0.5%); the day length (secondsPerDay = 86,400) is immutable — fixed at deploy so it can never re-price un-settled income.
