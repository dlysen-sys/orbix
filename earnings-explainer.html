ORBIX · Income Model

How members earn on ORBIX

Three income streams, straight from the live contracts — what a stake pays out, what referrals pay, and what the binary override pays leaders. Every figure below is computed from the deployed parameters.

Assumption for this walkthrough:  every member stakes 1,000 USDT
A

The three ways you earn

1

Staking passive — your own stake pays you a daily USDT yield, and returns your ORBIX principal after the lock. Paid to every staker.

2

Referral — a flat unilevel bonus (5% direct + 1% for five more levels) each time someone in your sponsor line stakes. Paid from the buffer.

3

Override — a daily two-leg passive on your binary group volume, scaled by your rank. The leadership income; paid from the buffer.

3

Override — the leadership income

daily, two legs

Your binary tree has a LEFT and a RIGHT leg. Each pays a daily passive on that leg's group volume, set by the formula:

daily(leg) = legVolume × 0.5% multiplier × ( yourRate − topDownlineRate )

yourRate = rank × 5% · the “− topDownlineRate” is the breakaway differential: you earn on the gap between your rank and your strongest downline's rank.

The rank ladder

To HOLD a rank you need all three: the qualifying group volume, the direct count, AND the per-rank min self-stake (your own open stake). Your rate = rank × 5%. The override / day is that row's own qualifying volume run through vol × 0.005 × rate — the max daily override the group generates at that rank.

▲
What you must MAINTAIN to earn — the safety net (applies to all three streams). Your override, referral, and passive all pay only while you hold a live open position: close your stake and every stream pauses until you re-stake (and a revive must be ≥ your prior stake). Your min self-stake gates your earning rank — withdraw below the floor for your rank and earning re-prices down to the rank your remaining stake backs (the title is kept, monotonic). One override: an admin can assign your rank directly, which beats the rules and earns even with no self-stake — your final rank is whichever is higher, the naturally-earned rank or the admin-assigned one.
