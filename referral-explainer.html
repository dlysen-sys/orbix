01 · How it pays

A flat rate on every stake beneath you

Referral rides your affiliate (sponsorship) tree — the chain of who invited whom. When anyone in your downline opens a USDT stake, the contract walks up from them and pays each generation a fixed percentage of that stake, in USDT, straight into a claimable balance.

1Direct sponsor — 5%

The person who invited the staker (level 1) earns directBps = 5% of the stake. The biggest single slice, for bringing them in.

5×Indirect — 1% each

The next five generations up (levels 2–6) each earn indirectBps = 1% — indirectLevels = 5. Depth rewards the people who built the network above you.

Σ10% reserved per stake

Total = 5% + 1%×5 = 10% (referralReserveBps). This exact slice is carved from the staker's own USDT — it is never a company subsidy.

◆ Requirement — you must stay staked to receive. An upline whose own position is closed (no live stake, and not admin-assigned a rank) is skipped — the walk continues past them to the next eligible upline, so referral is never paid to a lapsed member. In the contract, _payReferral skips any upline where activeStake == 0 && rankFloor == 0. The same safety net gates the override and the staking passive.

Rates are configurable by the program (setReferralPlan(direct, indirect, levels)) within the split invariant; the figures here are the marketing-plan defaults. If a generation above the staker is empty (no sponsor recorded), the walk simply stops there.

03 · Where the money comes from

Funded by the stake, held until you claim

The stake pays for itself

1Stake
A member opens a USDT stake of value V.
→
2Reserve
10% of V is set aside into the leadership pool as the referral slice.
→
3Pay up
The pool credits each upline's held referral balance up the affiliate tree.
  • Because the 10% comes from the staker's own USDT, referral is zero-sum — no subsidy pool to farm.
  • It fires on USDT-funded stakes (and company-reward stakes); pure-ORBIX personal stakes pay none.

Held first, then released

  • Each payout lands in your held referral balance (heldIncomeReferral) — tracked separately from your leadership override, so you always see where income came from.
  • Call releaseHeld() to move it (with any override income) to your withdrawable OrbixAssets vault balance.
  • The USDT stays backed in the pool until you release it; an admin sweep can never touch balances owed to members.
  • Referral pays regardless of your rank — you don't need to qualify for a leadership tier to earn it.
