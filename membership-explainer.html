01 · The subscription

One $20 charge, paid from your vault balance

Registration is free. Subscribing charges the fee from your OrbixAssets vault balance; any shortfall is drawn from a credit line an admin has granted you and recorded as debt. Members with a funded balance are unaffected.

$The fee

$20 USDT per subscription — first term or renewal. Charged via debitWithCredit(user, USDT, 20): spends balance first, borrows the rest.

⏱The term

Your first-ever subscription lasts 90 days — a 30-day base plus a one-time 60-day launch promo. Every renewal adds 30 days, stacked onto any time remaining.

↑Qualifying your sponsor

Your first-ever subscription bumps your sponsor's qualifiedDirects by one. That count is what unlocks their earning depth (next section). It never decreases.

02 · Where the $20 goes

Five allocations, conserved to the last cent

On every subscription the fee is split into five buckets by basis-point weights that must sum to 100% (the contract enforces liquidity + sys + community + unilevel + profit == BPS). Nothing is left over — unclaimed unilevel levels fall through to Profit.

The weights are fixed in the contract; only the fee scales. At the default $20 the buckets are $5 / $1 / $1 / $6 / $7. Change the fee to see each bucket recompute as fee × bps / 10000.

03 · Unilevel Leadership Payout · $6.00 (30%)

$0.30 per level, up to 20 levels deep

The $6 unilevel pool pays $0.30 per level — that's 1.5% of the $20 fee — up the binary tree (the compensation genealogy, not the referral tree), 20 levels max. But you only collect a level's $0.30 when both hold: you're an active subscriber, and you're qualified deep enough to reach it. Any level no qualified, active upline claims is forfeited to Profit.

The qualification ladder

Your reach is set by qualifiedDirects — your personally sponsored members who have subscribed at least once — through _maxLevel(). You qualify by sponsoring directs; you earn on your binary downline:

What "forfeited" means

  • Every level from 1 to 20 carries a $0.30 slice of a subscription's fee.
  • An upline that's lapsed (not subscribed) or under-qualified for that depth skips the slice — it's added to residual.
  • All residual is credited to the Profit wallet, so the $6 pool is always fully spent.
  • unilevelIncome tracks your lifetime earnings for the dashboard; the spendable balance lives in the vault.

04 · Community Rewards · $1.00 (5%)

The 20th-level reward, now staking savings

The $1 community slice is the 20th-level reward. On each subscription it goes to the member sitting on the subscriber's deepest (20th) level — if they're an active subscriber — as on-chain communitySavings, a per-member USDT balance backed by a dedicated pool. It isn't withdrawable: it exists to help members save toward staking.

Two landing spots — by exact rule

  • Active 20th-level upline exists → the $1 becomes their communitySavings (backed, staking-redeemable). SAVINGS
  • Tree shorter than 20, or that upline lapsed → the $1 falls back to the company communityWallet instead. FALLBACK

05 · Binary placement · technical

Where you land in the tree — and the depth cap

The Unilevel plan pays up the binary tree, so where a new member is placed matters. At registration you pick a placement mode and the contract resolves the exact open slot with getPlacement(sponsor, option) — the same walk register re-runs on-chain, so the preview can never disagree with the actual placement.

The sponsor is the top node

Placement always searches from the sponsor you enter (the binary top node), never above it:

  • Force Left — walk the left spine to the first open left slot.
  • Force Right — walk the right spine to the first open right slot.
  • Auto (weakest leg) — descend the leg carrying the lower volume (salesVol + personalVol), balancing the tree.

The depth cap — maxIteration = 20

The Force-Left / Force-Right spine walk (_binaryOpenNode) is bounded to 20 levels. If a spine is filled that deep, the walk stops and falls back to the weakest-leg walk (_binaryWeakLegNode) — it never keeps descending.

This bounds gas to ≤20 storage reads per placement and guarantees the search always terminates on a shallower open leg. Admin-tunable via setMaxIteration.

Why the 21st level is never reached

Take a fully-filled left spine 21 nodes deep and a Force-Left placement, cap at 20:

hop (guard < 20)nodeaction
0root · L0left filled → descend to L1

The walk descends at most 20 levels (to L20) and exits before it can even read L20's child, so level 21 (S21) is never reached. It hands off to the weakest-leg walk, which places at the sponsor's open right slot (L1). Foundry proof: test_openNode_falls_back_to_weakleg_at_maxIteration_20 — capped at 20 it returns (root, right); lift the cap and the same Force-Left reaches (S21, left).
