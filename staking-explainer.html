00 · What you get

Two return streams from one stake

A stake splits your return into two independent flows. The yield is paid in USDT while your money is locked; the principal is the ORBIX you were given, returned in full after the lock. They never mix.

$Passive USDT yield — during the lock

Your staked USDT value earns a fixed daily rate (your tier's dailyBps) from day one to the unlock date. Claim it into your wallet any day with claimPassive — this is your real income.

  • ~10–15% per month in USDT, by tier.
  • Accrues every day, freezes at unlock.
  • Claimable anytime the vault holds USDT surplus to cover it.

◇ORBIX principal — after the lock

The fixed ORBIX the contract locked for you is returned at 2.5% every 30 days over 40 months, starting 30 days after unlock. It never grows and never shrinks — it's your principal coming home.

  • A fixed ORBIX amount, priced when you stake.
  • Reserved in full up-front — always backed.
  • Withdrawn with claim, 2.5% at a time.
01 · The tiers

Three lock lengths, three daily yields

Each tier fixes a daily USDT yield (in bps of the USDT you staked) and a lock length, seeded in the contract's constructor. Longer locks pay a lower daily rate; every tier returns roughly 119–120% in USDT yield across its full lock. The rate is snapshotted onto your position — a later tier change never touches stakes already open.

03 · How it accrues

Yield during the lock, principal after it

$Passive USDT — during the lock

  • Accrues at your tier's daily rate on the USDT you staked: usdtPrincipal × dailyBps × days / BPS.
  • Runs from your stake date and freezes at unlock — the lock is the earning window.
  • The rate is snapshotted at stake time (position.dailyBps), immune to later tier edits.
  • Claim any day via claimPassive(index); each claim pays up to the vault's USDT surplus, and any remainder stays claimable after a top-up.

◇ORBIX principal — after the lock

  • Your fixed ORBIX principal returns at 2.5% every 30 days (vestingBps = 250), snapshotted per position.
  • The schedule runs from the unlock date — the first 2.5% lands 30 days after unlock, then every 30 days.
  • Full principal returns over 40 months (100% ÷ 2.5%); claim each slice via claim(index).
  • It never grows and never shrinks — this is a withdrawal of your own ORBIX, metered out, not a reward.
04 · Funding a stake

You bring USDT — the contract brings the ORBIX

You never have to go buy ORBIX first. The staking contract holds a project-funded ORBIX reserve that supplies your fixed principal, reserved up-front when you stake. Several ways in — all end in the same locked position.

APay with USDT

stake(tier, usdt) — the reserve hands you ORBIX at the LP's TWAP price; your USDT is routed to the leadership pool, liquidity, and profit (the split below). This is the earning path — it sets your passive USDT basis and pays your uplines.

BStake ORBIX you hold

stakeWithOrbix(tier, amount) — lock ORBIX already in your vault balance as principal. Principal-only: pays no upline rewards. stakeWithOrbixRewards adds company-funded leadership rewards.

CAuth & promo grants

stakeByAuth* — an admin can open a stake for a member (charged or free-from-reserve), with or without leadership rewards. The company-promotion path.

Where a USDT stake's money goes

A USDT stake's own USDT is split across the ecosystem (deployed config; retunable within caps):

Leadership pool 50% Liquidity 30% Referral 10% Profit 10%

The leadership + referral slices fund the override + referral payouts (see the companion pages). The split is enforced to total ≤ 100% by setStakeConfig + setReferralPlan.

05 · Built to be safe

Where your value lives — and what can't touch it

The staking contract was audited and hardened this build. The properties below are the ones that protect a staker's funds.

🔒Reserved principal

Your fixed ORBIX principal is moved to the contract's own vault account and stays tracked — admin surplus-sweeps can never spend it. Every scheduled return always has it to give back.

⛓Immutable wiring

The accounts and assets contracts are fixed at deploy — no admin can re-point staking at a malicious registry or vault.

📸Snapshotted terms

Your daily yield rate and 2.5% return rate are frozen onto the position when you stake, so a later parameter change can't rewrite a live obligation.

$Passive is surplus-gated

Each claimPassive pays only up to the vault's live USDT surplus — it can never spend USDT that backs other obligations. Any unpaid remainder stays claimable once the surplus is topped up, so a claim is always solvent.

⏸Pause & funding gates

Staking, claims, and returns can be paused in an emergency. USDT-funded stakes need enough reserve; if it can't fill, the stake reverts cleanly rather than half-completing.

⚖The group-sales withdrawal gate

If you took a USDT credit line to stake, your principal returns are held until your downline group sales reach creditUsed × 5 (checked against the leadership module) — or you repay the debt. Held ORBIX is still reserved for you the whole time; releaseHeld frees it once you qualify. Cash stakers are never gated.
