C

Why the override is the hard part

Override differentials telescope up each chain — every level earns the gap to the one below, and the gaps sum to the top rank. So the total override paid on a unit of volume is simply:

total override / day = volume × multiplier × top rank rate

At the current 0.5% multiplier with Rank-5 (25%) leaders on top: 0.125% of volume / day — that's ≈ 45.6% of the volume per year, and it doesn't stop (volume is monotonic; it only winds down through breakaway).

◆
Higher-ranked tops make it worse: a Rank-10 (50%) top = 0.25%/day = ~91%/year of volume. Lower ranks or breakaway make it better. The number below assumes Rank-5 tops as a mid-case.

At the current 0.5% multiplier the override would need the trading desk to return ~114% per year — a stretch, not comfortable. To fund the override from realistic trading profit (say 20–25% / year), the multiplier needs to be around 0.1% (10 bps) — roughly a 5× reduction from today's 50 bps. That is the single dial that makes the model self-funding.

Even setting the override aside, trading must roughly triple its 40% allocation over the lock to cover the passive. Because passive is capped per stake and paid over time (and claims only pay up to the vault surplus), it's manageable with steady tops-ups — the override is the part that needs the multiplier dialed down.

How these are derived: override total = volume × multiplier × top-rank-rate (differentials telescope to the top rank); shown at 1,000,000 staked with Rank-5 (25%) tops and full differential (no breakaway) — a mid ceiling. Passive = 0.33%/day × 360 days = 118.8% per Starter stake. Referral 10% is self-funded by the buffer's referral reserve. Trading ROI needed = annual override ÷ 400,000 trading fund.

Real override sits below these ceilings once downlines rank up (breakaway shrinks every differential). Claims pay only what the buffer / vault surplus can currently back, so a shortfall defers payouts rather than bricking staking — but deferred payouts are still owed. The multiplier is live-tunable (setOverrideMultiplier); the day clock is fixed at deploy.
